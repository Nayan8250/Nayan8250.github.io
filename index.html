About Me

I am interested in experimental physics, precision measurement, interferometry, control systems, FPGA-based instrumentation, gravitational-wave instrumentation, and astrophysics. My work includes the development and testing of digital signal processing and control systems using the Red Pitaya STEMlab platform.

Selected Projects

Laser RIN Measurement

Measurement and analysis of laser relative intensity noise using Red Pitaya, photodetectors, PSD estimation and multi-band spectral analysis.

FPGA Phase-Locked Loop

FPGA-based PLL development using IQ demodulation, digital filtering, PID feedback and Red Pitaya.

IQ Demodulator & PID

Custom SystemVerilog implementation of IQ signal processing and digital control for precision frequency and phase experiments.

Frequency-to-Voltage Converter

FPGA-based frequency measurement and conversion of frequency information into a proportional DAC voltage.

Adaptive Dual-Input F-to-V Converter

Dual-input signed frequency-difference measurement system for high-speed frequency-error detection and feedback control.

Wideband Beat-Frequency Monitoring

Development of a wideband FPGA monitoring path for observing beat frequencies from low frequency to the MHz range.

Research Interests

Gravitational-wave instrumentation, precision interferometry, laser stabilization, FPGA-based digital control, phase-locked loops, signal processing, astronomical instrumentation and astrophysics.
