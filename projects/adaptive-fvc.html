Adaptive Dual-Input Frequency-to-Voltage Converter

FPGA implementation on the Red Pitaya STEMlab 125-14 for real-time, signed frequency-difference measurement and conversion into a proportional analog voltage.

Red Pitaya FPGA SystemVerilog Frequency Measurement Digital Control

Project Overview

This project was developed as a faster alternative to a conventional reciprocal frequency-counter-based frequency-to-voltage converter. Two input signals are measured simultaneously and their signed frequency difference is calculated in real time.

The measured frequency error is

Δf = fIN2 − fIN1

This signed frequency difference is converted into a proportional DAC voltage and provided at the Red Pitaya analog output.

Hardware

Red Pitaya STEMlab 125-14, two signal generators, oscilloscope, and FPGA logic implemented in Vivado.
